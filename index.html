Permissions Explained
Accessibility Services
Accessibility permission required

🤖 Enable MYRA AI to control your phone through voice commands. This permission allows reading screen content and performing actions for hands-free device automation and improved accessibility.

🤖 Why MYRA Needs Accessibility Services:
MYRA is an AI assistant that helps you control your phone through voice commands. To do this, it requires accessibility permissions to understand and interact with your device.

📱 What This Permission Allows:

• Read Screen Content: MYRA can see and analyze what's currently displayed on your screen to understand the context and provide relevant assistance.
• Perform Actions: Execute touch gestures, taps, swipes, and navigation (Back, Home, Recent Apps) on your behalf based on your voice commands.
• App Interaction: Open apps, fill forms, scroll through content, and navigate between different screens as requested.

🔒 Privacy & Data Protection:

• All processing happens on Gemini API
• No personal data is collected or stored, but it is sent to Google Gemini
• Screen content is only accessed when you actively use MYRA, otherwise MYRA doesn't access screen content
• We implement safeguards to protect sensitive information, but MYRA can still make mistakes

♿ Accessibility Benefits:
This service enables hands-free device control, making smartphones more accessible for users with disabilities, visual impairments, or mobility limitations.

By proceeding, you understand and consent to MYRA using accessibility services for AI-powered device automation.
